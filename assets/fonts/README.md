# Fonts

Two typefaces a reader can choose in the front page's reading settings, self-hosted so that the page works without reaching a font service.

- **Atkinson Hyperlegible**, by the Braille Institute of America, designed for readers with low vision. Distributed under the Atkinson Hyperlegible Font License.
- **OpenDyslexic**, by Abbie Gonzalez, designed for readers with dyslexia. Distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The eight `.woff2` files (regular, bold, and their italics, Latin subset) and `../fonts.css` are copied from dewlab's `assets/vendor/`, which took them from the Fontsource builds. Neither typeface is modified.
