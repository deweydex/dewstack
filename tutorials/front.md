Welcome to dewstack. Here we learn to build websites and work with data.
You can read the tutorials without an account or anything to install.
The website exercises use a free GitHub account. The data exercises run
in your browser without an account.

You can begin with [a website](tutorial:how-the-pieces-fit) or
[a table of data](tutorial:a-table-is-a-list-of-rows). If you are learning
in class, your teacher can help you find where to begin. If you are
learning on your own, either path is available.

## Learning here

Each tutorial explains an idea and offers something to try. We can begin
with an example, change one thing, and look at the result.

Sometimes the result will be different from what we expected. This can
help us decide what to try next. If you feel stuck, [the troubleshooting
page](tutorial:troubleshooting) has suggestions. You can also ask your
teacher for help, or pause and return later.

The tutorials and exercises are not graded. The college grades the two
projects and the exam. Your teacher explains those tasks in class.

## Your work and your reading settings

Your website files are kept on GitHub when you save them there. On the
data pages, a box labelled **Your table** saves its SQL text in this
browser when you use **Run** or **Load**. Other SQL boxes do not save
your changes between visits. **Download** gives you a copy of the SQL
text to keep. The [FAQ](tutorial:faq) explains more about saving work.

The **Settings** button lets you change the colours, font, text size,
and line width. Your choices are saved in this browser.

The tutorials below are grouped by subject. Within each group, the pages
are listed in teaching order. The reference pages offer help with common
problems and reminders of what code means.

Teachers and anyone interested in how the course is organised can read
[the README on GitHub](https://github.com/deweydex/dewstack#readme).
