Irish Tax Simulator (Real Distribution)

This simulator uses Revenue's 2023 individualized gross income distribution to estimate the number of earners in each income band and their total income. Adjust the sliders to change the income tax brackets, rates, credits and PRSI rate. The charts will update to show total revenue and the effective tax rate across incomes.
